# "NEWS YOU CAN USE" — 20 YOUTUBE SHORTS PACK

**Channel concept:** Current news + studies turned into actionable moves — get rich, get ripped, get ahead.
**Format:** 60–90 second vertical Shorts. Punchy hook (3 sec) → value (45–70 sec) → CTA (5–10 sec).
**Research basis:** Real headlines and studies from April 2026.
**Deliverable includes:** 20 scripts · thumbnail concepts · on-screen text cues · source links · 7-day upload schedule · production checklist.

---

## HOW TO USE THIS FILE

Each video has:
1. **Title** — what appears on YouTube
2. **Hook (0–3s)** — the first sentence you say on camera; this is 80% of whether it works
3. **Script** — word-for-word what to say, timed for ~75 seconds
4. **On-screen text** — bold captions to overlay for silent viewers (80% of Shorts are watched muted)
5. **B-roll cues** — what to show visually while you talk
6. **Thumbnail** — the single frame that sells the click
7. **Sources** — so every claim is defensible
8. **Hashtags** — 3–5 max (YouTube penalizes spam)

**Production stack (cheap + fast):**
- Film on iPhone, vertical 4K 60fps, good lighting (ring light or window)
- Edit: CapCut (free) — add captions, zooms, B-roll, sound effects
- AI voiceover option: ElevenLabs (if you want to stay off camera for some videos)
- AI B-roll option: Veo / Runway / Pika for dramatic stock footage
- Music: YouTube Audio Library (royalty-free)
