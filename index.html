Top questions

How is the cancellation fee calculated?You can cancel for free within the first 2 minutes of booking. After that, $5 applies once your barber is on the way, $10 if they've arrived, and $25 once the cut has started — same idea as Uber's rider cancellation policy.
What if my barber doesn't show up?If your barber doesn't arrive within 10 minutes of the promised ETA, the appointment is cancelled at no cost and we automatically match you with another verified pro nearby.
Are the barbers background-checked?Every Catch a Fade pro passes ID verification, a criminal background check and a portfolio review. Look for the "Verified Pro" badge on their profile.
How does tipping work?You can pre-tip at booking or tip after the cut on the rating screen. 100% of every tip goes directly to your barber.
What's the PIN for?When your barber arrives, you'll see a 4-digit PIN. Share it with them — they'll enter it in their app to confirm they're at the right address. Same as Uber's pickup PIN.
How does surge pricing work?When demand is higher than the number of available barbers (Saturday nights, holidays, etc.), prices go up a small multiplier to bring more barbers online. You'll always see the multiplier before you confirm.
Can I request the same barber again?Yes — add them to your favorites from their profile or the rating screen. Catch a Fade+ members get priority scheduling with favorite barbers.
